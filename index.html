Free-Form Insight 1
Delivery contacts persist even when resolved
Missing items, split shipments, delayed replacements - even when agents fix these, customers contact again. Proactive order tracking notifications would prevent this category entirely.
Free-Form Insight 2

Bundle purchases, gifts, missing serial numbers - these create confusion and repeat contacts even when agents do everything right. The registration process has a structural complexity problem training alone cannot fix.
